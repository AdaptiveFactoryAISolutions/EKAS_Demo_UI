3. Read the displayed scope, role, cutoff, procedure and step. Click **Guide me** to start an optional walkthrough for that context. Starting it does not run a business command.
4. Click **Show me**. It may navigate to an existing page, focus a control, or open a permitted read-only evidence panel. If it navigates, wait for the page context and use Show me again. It never enters inputs, approves work, exports a file or confirms a proposal for you.

7. **Close Guide** or Escape closes it. Closing/pause is not cancellation of an already-submitted business request. **Observed Guide steps** distinguishes read-only inspections from normal-service receipts; neither establishes a cause automatically.

## Blocked actions and recovery

- No open case or wrong dataset: explicitly open the intended offered case. Help on a comparison/training cursor must not silently substitute a retained manufacturing case.
- Owner requested but not accepted: change to maintenance and use the acceptance button. Recommending or approving cannot substitute for acceptance.
- No hypothesis/test proposal: author and inspect it before recommendation. A proposed test does not count as a performed result.
- Wrong role: choose the role appropriate to the next command and wait for any active request to settle. A role name typed in prose is not authority.
- Approval recorded, work absent: advance intentionally to the applicable inspection snapshot, inspect exact work proof, then record it as maintenance.
- Verification incomplete: inspect required eligible shifts, exact endpoint, source freshness and performed proof. Do not advance or change counts solely to manufacture a pass.
- Safety required: review it as safety before manager acceptance. An early Accept result refusal is expected protection.
- Stale/missing source or changed version: read the current source/version and qualification; restate the intended proposal. Do not use historical answers as current or turn missing into zero.
- Guide paused: use Resume Guide / revalidate; restore the original context or Restart for current context. Reading/skipping does not complete missing work.
- Interrupted/unknown request: read durable state/receipt before retrying. Do not clear protected storage or accept a workspace reset. Recovery gates and broader adverse paths are not fully accepted.

Source corrections and governed reviews require exact versions, human reason, known UTC and inspection of the staged proposal before confirmation. Cancel writes nothing. A current count alone does not select raw cohort units. Controlled claim quotes/relationships remain distinct from facts and causal conclusions. Six value stages retain their units/horizons; observed gains are not verified financial savings.
