# 01 Overview

EKAS helps a plant leader go from a missed operating goal to evidence, a governed decision, accountable action, verification, and sustained improvement. The conversation is the primary interface. Dashboards, investigations, and evidence are the same case seen from another surface, not a second product.

## What this demo is

The accepted package is EKAS DEMO RC3, source `45568c575a00a8810b661929624995ec24e6de1e`. Chat runs on governed local rules over synthetic fixtures. Numbers such as the Northworks plan miss of 150 good units, and the Line 03 contribution of 138, are fixture facts. They are not a live plant feed.

## What this demo is not

- It is not a generic charting tool. A chart that cannot name its case, scope, and source does not answer the plant question.
- It is not MES. MES executes and records. EKAS interprets, connects evidence, governs decisions, and verifies outcomes.
- It is not an approved production deployment. Adapter Verified is NO. Live Integration Verified is NO. Final release is not approved.

## How a session moves

A person asks in Chat. EKAS keeps the plant, line, period, metric, and case that the conversation has established. A pronoun such as “it” uses that referent. An explicit new topic replaces it. A new conversation clears it. When more than one case is still valid, EKAS asks which one.

The plant-review checkpoint is: run the review, ask what to focus on first, then say “Investigate it.” The recommended focus is the Northworks feeder-jam plan miss. The investigation that opens is that case.
