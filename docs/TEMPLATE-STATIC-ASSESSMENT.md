- Catalog entries must carry units, formula definitions, aggregation rules and their versions. Context must explicitly bind plant, asset, manufactured part/revision, job, operation, shift, timezone and snapshot. Comparison eligibility and exclusions belong to the canonical result, not merely a chart label.
- Canonical Line03 OEE is75% availability ×90% performance ×97.2222…% quality =65.625%. Retain underlying precision and pooled numerator/denominator aggregation. Never average displayed OEE percentages. UnsupportedP3 remains unavailable; P2 must not be substituted.
- Model result states distinctly: observed zero, no-data, zero denominator, masked, unsupported and invalid formula. Return explicit reason codes and availability rather than coalescing states into0, empty strings or a generic error.
- Answer and tile evidence must bind the exact result, context, definition version and snapshot, with source anchors/eligibility. Do not represent fake SQL as executed provenance. Query, table, chart and headline consume one canonical result; chart adapters format it without recalculation or divergent filtering.
- Govern formula operands, compatible units, permitted operations, masking propagation and denominator eligibility. Save formula/query versions. Invalid dimensional arithmetic, missing operands or zero denominators must be withheld with reasons; masking must not be circumvented by derived values.
- Board verification is a human definition/content marker bound to the specific definition/content version, actor and time. Editing the verified content must disclose that the marker refers to an earlier version. It never establishes cause, performed intervention, effectiveness or financial savings.
- Eliminate hidden80-group caps. Any real truncation must show actual limit, returned count, eligible total when known and truncation status. Do not claim completeness without evidence.
- Saved queries must distinguish reproduction of the original snapshot from an explicit refresh. Refresh creates a new result/version with its own context, definition, snapshot and eligibility; it must not silently rewrite captured answers or tiles as current.
- Demo role switches confer no security authority. Monitors, schedules and authorization remain explicitly simulated/unavailable and not dispatched. No background evaluation, delivery, sharing grant or webhook promise is implied.
