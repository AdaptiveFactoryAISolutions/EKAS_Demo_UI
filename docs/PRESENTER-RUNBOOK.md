## Value discussion and sensitivity

The proposal is to shorten the time from a manufacturing question to an evidence-qualified owned action and a verified operational observation. No price, funding request, validated savings or return guarantee is asserted here. For an illustrative business case, collect investigation count/month, minutes saved/investigation, loaded labor rate, eligible shift count, observed jam reduction/shift, realized constraint contribution/minute, implementation cost and recurring operating cost from the business owner.

Illustrative annual labor opportunity = investigations/month × minutes saved ×12 /60 × loaded hourly rate. Illustrative annual throughput opportunity = eligible shifts/year × attributable recovered constraint minutes/shift × realized contribution/minute. Avoid double counting labor already included in contribution; recovered minutes with no demand or no bottleneck realization do not create booked cash. Net modeled opportunity subtracts approved annual operating cost. Payback = approved implementation cost / positive net monthly realized benefit; report unavailable if the denominator is nonpositive or unsupported.

Show low/base/high assumptions for demand realization, eligible exposure, attributable fraction, labor adoption and operating cost.
