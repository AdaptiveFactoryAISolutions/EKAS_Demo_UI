# Claim and positioning — EKAS DEMO RC1

EKAS is one manufacturing expert you can talk to. Chat is the product. Dashboards, investigations, evidence, improvements, and knowledge answer the same conversation. They are not a separate BI suite.

What the local demo can claim:

- A plant leader can ask about a miss and follow one case from the gap through owner, authorization, performed work, verification, sustainment, and reopen.
- Plant operations and Production and OEE use the same context as Chat. The plant shortfall is 150 good units. Line 03 contributed 138 of that shortfall. Theoretical capacity loss is a separate figure.
- The presentation is light by default, with dark as an option. Icons are Lucide. Charts use Highcharts' own palette. Governed status is the only semantic color.

What the local demo must not claim:

- A live connection to current EKAS backend main.
- A verified adapter.
- A real provider conversation. None was run.
- Financial savings, a confirmed cause, or a P&L result that the records do not contain.
- That Plant Review discovery has completed an operational repair when the reply says that proof is absent.

The PLC-to-P&L story is a future reference. It is not this demo.
